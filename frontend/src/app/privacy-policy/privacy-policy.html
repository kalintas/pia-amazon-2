At Amazon 2, we value your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details (name, email).
  • Order history, cart data and preferences.
  • Technical data (IP, device, browser).

2. How We Use Information

  • To process orders and provide support.
  • To improve products, UX and security.
  • To send optional marketing (with consent; you can opt-out anytime).

3. Data Security

We use encryption in transit, strict access controls, and regular security reviews to protect your data.

4. Data Sharing

We only share data with payment/shipping providers to fulfill your orders and with analytics tools in anonymized form. We do not sell personal data.

5. Your Rights

Request access, correction, deletion, or objection to processing via privacy@amazon2.com.
